                                              Bring Commons to your county

                                              County administrators can register their school division and name an administrator for each school. The Commons team reviews every sign-up before any accounts are turned on.

                                              Thanks! is waiting for review.

                                              County details

                                              County administrator

                                              Email domains

                                              Enter the email domains your staff and students use, the part after the @. Separate more than one with commas. When your county is approved, these domains are authorized automatically: staff domains sign in as Teachers and student domains as Students.

                                              School administrators

                                              Add each school that will use Commons and the person who will manage it there. They get administrator access when your county is approved.

                                              What happens next

                                              1. The Commons team reviews your county's sign-up.
                                              2. Once approved, your email domains are authorized and your schools appear on the sign-in screen.
                                              3. You, your school administrators, teachers, and students sign in with their school email or Google.

                                              Already approved?

                                              Sign in with the email and password you used when you signed up.
